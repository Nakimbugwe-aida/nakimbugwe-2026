About EduConnect Uganda

EduConnect Uganda is an educational platform designed to provide learners with useful information about courses, learning resources and educational opportunities.

Our Mission

To connect learners with useful educational information and learning opportunities.

Our Vision

To become a useful digital platform supporting access to education and learning resources in Uganda.

Our Objectives

  1. Provide useful educational information.
  2. Connect students with learning opportunities.
  3. Promote digital learning.
  4. Provide access to learning resources.
  5. Support students in making informed educational choices.

Our Main Focus Areas

The table below summarises the work of EduConnect Uganda.

EduConnect Uganda Focus Areas
